Who Are We?

Arena Gaming CI is the undisputed leader of gaming in Côte d'Ivoire. Since our creation, we have established ourselves as the reference in the universe of video games and Ivorian esports.

With multiple experiences in the gaming sector, our passionate team combines technical expertise and in-depth knowledge of the local gaming community. We have developed a solid reputation thanks to our constant commitment to quality and innovation.

Our mission is to democratize access to quality gaming in Côte d'Ivoire, by offering modern infrastructure, cutting-edge equipment and professional services adapted to the needs of all players, from casual gamers to esports competitors.

Our Vision

To make Côte d'Ivoire a recognized gaming hub in West Africa, where every enthusiast can develop their talent and live their passion in the best conditions.

Our Values

  • Excellence: We always offer the best equipment and superior quality services
  • Passion: Gaming is our passion, we share it with our community
  • Innovation: We stay at the forefront of the latest gaming technologies
  • Community: We create lasting bonds between Ivorian gamers
